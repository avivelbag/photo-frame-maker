# Space frame

120 × 120 mm space-themed photo frame for the Bambu P1S. Everything is generated by `space_frame.py` (manifold3d + shapely). `preview.py` renders top-down images into `preview/`.

![front](preview/frame_front.png)

## Parts (`stl/`)

| File | What | Print orientation |
|---|---|---|
| `frame.stl` | frame + artwork, one colour | back face down, as exported |
| `frame_body.stl` + `frame_relief.stl` | same frame split for two colours | load both together → "single object with multiple parts", assign colours |
| `back_plate.stl` | snap-in back with *PER ASPERA* engraved | as exported (catches print on the bed side) |
| `moon_stand.stl` | cratered lunar hill desk stand, 12° lean | as exported |

No supports needed. The 2 mm lip over the photo pocket has a 45° taper underneath, and the overhang is hidden behind the photo anyway.

## Photo

- Visible window: 82 × 82 mm.
- Cut the photo to **87 × 87 mm**.
- The pocket holds 2.2 mm in front of the back plate. Fill it with the photo plus a 1 mm clear sheet or a piece of card so nothing rattles.
- The back plate snaps in with two flexing edge catches. Pop it out with a fingernail at the notch on the bottom edge.

## Hanging / standing

- Keyhole on the back top border fits a screw with a head up to 7 mm and a shank up to 3.6 mm.
- Or stand it in `moon_stand.stl`.

## Colour without an AMS

The body ends at exactly **z = 8.0 mm**. Add a filament change at the first layer above 8.0 mm (Bambu Studio: right-click the layer slider → *Add pause* / *Change filament*). For example, navy body with gold or white artwork.

## Suggested settings

0.2 mm layers (0.12 mm for crisper stars), 3 walls, 15% infill, PLA. Smooth PEI or textured plate is fine (the frame prints back-down).

## Regenerate

```
pip install manifold3d shapely numpy matplotlib
python space_frame.py && python preview.py
```
